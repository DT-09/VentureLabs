# VentureLabs — paid validation report

## What it is

A focused, evidence-led review for a founder facing a specific venture decision. The output is a concise report that separates facts from assumptions, identifies the riskiest unresolved claims, and recommends the next experiment.

It is designed to help a founder decide what to test next—not to promise that a startup will succeed.

## Suitable questions

- Which customer segment should we test first?
- Is this problem frequent or costly enough to justify a pilot?
- What evidence would distinguish a real buying need from polite interest?
- Which assumption could invalidate this idea?
- What is the smallest useful test before investing in a build?

## What the buyer receives

- A written commercial thesis and decision question.
- A structured evidence ledger with sources and evidence status.
- Critical assumptions ranked by decision impact and uncertainty.
- Contradictions, gaps, and alternative explanations.
- A practical experiment plan with success and failure thresholds.
- A recommendation and next actions, with limitations stated clearly.

## Scope and process

1. Agree on the decision, inputs, timeline, deliverable, payment terms, and data handling.
2. Review the founder-provided material.
3. Separate observed evidence from assumptions and interpretation.
4. Identify the decision-critical uncertainties.
5. Produce the report and allow one factual-correction pass.

Independent research, source checking, customer interviews, or additional revisions are included only if explicitly agreed and actually performed.

## What it does not include by default

- A guarantee of revenue, funding, product-market fit, or startup success.
- Legal, tax, investment, or regulatory advice.
- Customer outreach or interviews.
- Independently verified market sizing or competitor analysis.
- A claim that the report's conclusions are statistically proven.

## Commercial terms

The fee and timeline are agreed for the actual scope before work starts. No custom analysis begins until the scope and payment arrangement are confirmed. The buyer should not share confidential third-party data or personal information unless they have permission and the handling arrangement is appropriate.

## Call to action

Bring one live decision, the evidence already available, and the date by which you need to decide. The first step is to confirm whether the decision and available inputs fit this report format.
