02 / The method

Reduce uncertainty in the right order.

Good validation is not a high score. It is a sequence of tests that can disprove a weak idea before the team spends heavily.

01

Pin down the pain

Define a narrow customer, the triggering event, current workaround, and observable cost. Avoid broad markets and vague aspirations.

Problem evidence
02

Find the economic buyer

Determine who owns the budget, what they already pay for, and what would cause them to switch or act now.

Commercial evidence
03

Run the smallest test

Use a structured interview, manual service, prototype walkthrough, or paid pilot with a clear pass/fail threshold.

Behavioral evidence
03 / Working loop

From assumption to a decision.

Keep the next step small, observable, and tied to a decision—not a feature list.

STEP 01

Write the hypothesis

Who has which problem, in what situation, with what consequence?

STEP 02

Rank uncertainty

Find the assumption that would most damage the venture if false.

STEP 03

Design a test

Choose a signal, sample, deadline, and explicit success threshold.

STEP 04

Decide honestly

Proceed, revise, or stop based on what happened—not sunk cost.
